FFmpeg 7.1.5 — GNU GPL version 3 or later for this build.
The executable invokes FFmpeg as a separate process.
Source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.5.tar.xz
Build configuration and exact archive hashes are in build-ffmpeg.sh and
ffmpeg-source-sha256.txt. For the macOS build, the script refreshes
the GNU host detection scripts in LAME and libvorbis from libogg.
It also removes a legacy linker flag from libvorbis configure.
The FFmpeg build statically includes these libraries:
